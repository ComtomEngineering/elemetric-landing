KEY MARKET FACTORS

Why did the market fluctuate over these 90 days?

The renewable generation mix and the international cost of natural gas determine the final price of each hour in the OMIE pool.

local_fire_department WINTER GAS SPIKES

Gas drives the evening peaks

Heating demand pushes gas (MIBGAS) prices up exactly when electricity use peaks: the 18–22h slot in November keeps hitting new highs, with a 380 €/MWh spike on Nov 4 at 20:00.

Direct impact: +68% period average vs the previous quarter
air WIND: ZERO-PRICE DAYS

21 days at zero or negative price

Wind production pushed the pool price to 0 €/MWh (or below) on a quarter of the days in the period, even in late autumn. The cheapest day was Oct 18, averaging just 76.3 €/MWh.

Direct impact: up to -100% in windy slots
bolt OFF-PEAK NOT CHEAP

This time the night doesn't pay off

Normally the off-peak (night) slot is the cheapest. This period it's the opposite: off-peak 0.217 €/kWh vs mid-peak 0.201 and peak 0.209. With nighttime heating demand and no solar output, charging your home or car 'at night' no longer guarantees savings.

Direct impact: off-peak costs ~8% more than mid-peak this period
GUIDE & SAVING TIPS NO-COST ACTIONS

How to cut your electricity bill

Concrete measures to pay less for the same consumption, applicable to the 2.0TD tariff.

nightlight

Shift consumption to off-peak hours

Schedule your washing machine, dishwasher, water heater and EV charging between 00:00 and 08:00 on weekdays, or any time on weekends. The P3 band is the cheapest of the day.

schedule

Avoid peak hours

The critical windows are 10:00–14:00 and 18:00–22:00 on weekdays. Don't run your most powerful appliances there.

power

Review your contracted power

You pay for the contracted kW even if you don't use them. If your home doesn't draw all of it, lower it and save every month without changing your habits.

compare_arrows

Compare PVPC against free-market tariffs

Once a year, compare your current tariff with fixed free-market offers for your real consumption profile. The winner changes over time.

lightbulb

Cut standby consumption

TVs, routers and chargers draw power even when off. Unplug them or use switches on power strips, and replace incandescent bulbs with LED.

receipt_long

Audit your bill

Check your CUPS code, contracted power and energy charges with Elemetric's free calculator to spot fees you shouldn't be paying.

General averages do not reflect your exact habits. Upload your bill as PDF or enter your peak/shoulder/valley hours to audit which supplier would charge you less today.
